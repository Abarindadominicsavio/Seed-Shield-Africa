Our Mission & Context

The Challenge & Vision

Sub-Saharan Africa’s agricultural economies face severe vulnerabilities and crop failures driven by compounding climate shocks, fragile monocultures, and the rapid disappearance of traditional farmer varieties. Across Western Uganda, smallholder farmers also experience devastating post-harvest losses due to inadequate drying infrastructure and humidity exposure.

Seed Shield Africa bridges decentralized village seed vaults, solar drying post-harvest technologies, quantitative crop phenotyping, climate-smart intercropping packages, and immersive eco-agritourism to restore soil health, safeguard indigenous agrobiodiversity, and secure regional food sovereignty.

Operational Architecture

Our Core Pillars

A dual-impact social enterprise uniting grassroots conservation, post-harvest engineering, and scientific rigor.

1. Decentralized Village Seed Vaults

Establishing community-governed seed banks utilizing low-cost hermetic PICS bags and silica gel desiccation (maintaining 8–12% moisture control) across Ibanda, Kabarole, and Rubirizi to preserve climate-resilient traditional landraces.

2. Solar Driers & Post-Harvest Tech

Equipping smallholder cooperatives with advanced solar driers and hermetic storage solutions to rapidly eliminate grain moisture, prevent aflatoxin contamination, and reduce post-harvest losses by 50%.

3. Quantitative Phenotyping & MET

Partnering with Makerere University (MAKCSID) and NARO/ZARDIs to execute multi-environment trials (MET), R/Python biometric evaluations, and climate-smart intercropping pairing native grains with medicinal trees.

4. Eco-Agritourism & Heritage

Monetizing conservation through experiential eco-agritourism hubs featuring guided native plant walking trails, interactive village seed vault tours, and traditional heritage culinary dining.
